How it works

Three steps. One button.

From the moment you hit Listen, capture and transcription happen on your Mac — only transcript text is sent off, to your own AI provider, to research.

01

Listen

One button captures your microphone and your Mac's system audio at once — both sides of any call, with no bot joining the meeting.

02

Transcribe on-device

Apple's on-device speech recognition turns talk into a clean, live transcript. Words appear in grey, then firm up into sentences. Audio is never stored.

03

Surface insight

Advisor decides what's worth helping with and slides in a card — an answer, background, or a fact-check — anchored to the exact moment it relates to.

Everything it does

A quiet, capable companion.

Proactive cards

Answers, background context, and fact-checks appear on their own — anchored to the moment they relate to.

Captures both sides

Your mic and your Mac's system audio at once — no meeting bot. Mute yourself mid-call and the room keeps transcribing.

On-device & private

Speech-to-text runs locally with Apple SpeechAnalyzer. Audio never leaves your Mac — nothing is recorded, only transcripts kept.

✦

Ask anything

Pose a question any time — about the conversation or beyond it. Or right-click any line to answer, explain, fact-check, or fix a mis-heard word.

Sessions browser

Past sessions in portable markdown — grouped by day or your own folders, searchable, tagged with coloured pills, renamable, and exportable.

Your notes & tools

Researches across web search, your Obsidian vault, and connectors like Jira, Confluence, and Slack — read-only by default.

Summarise on demand

One tap distils the whole conversation into a Summary card — live or on a saved session — and it's kept with the note.

Focus on the cards

Hide the transcript with one tap to watch just the cards stream in — the assistant's output and nothing else.

Floating & resumable

Keep the window on top beside your call. Stop pauses without losing context; Resume picks the session right back up.

Notes in the flow

Right-click any line to add your own note to the transcript. The assistant treats it as authoritative context, and it's kept with the session.

Folders with context

Record straight into a folder. Each folder can carry its own default context, so sessions started there begin already briefed.

Aあ

Speaks your language

Choose the transcription locale from Apple's supported list, or leave it on System default and it follows your Mac.

Every feature, in detail
Privacy by architecture

Single user. No accounts. No server of ours. Your audio never leaves the Mac.

To research, Advisor sends transcript text — never audio — to your own AI provider, Claude or Codex, on your existing subscription. There, agents are sandboxed by a per-call policy: web search and read-only access to non-sensitive files. No shell, no writes. Credential paths are denied; connectors fail closed.

Local-first
Transcription is on-device; listening is always explicitly user-initiated.
Sandboxed research
Per-call tool policy; ~/.ssh, ~/.aws and keychains denied.
Uses your own login
Research runs on your own Claude or Codex subscription — transcript text goes to your provider, never to us.
