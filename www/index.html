A live transcript that looks things up

Advisor transcribes conversations on your Mac and adds answers, background and fact-checks to the transcript as they happen.

Download for Mac

Free · macOS 26 · Apple Silicon

How it works

  1. Listen

    Press Listen. Advisor captures your microphone and your Mac's audio, so it hears both sides of a call. Nothing joins the meeting.

  2. Transcribe

    Apple's on-device speech recognition writes a live transcript. No audio is saved.

  3. Look things up

    When a question, a name or a claim comes up, Advisor adds a card with an answer, background or a fact-check, and cites its sources.

Every session is kept

Sessions are saved as Markdown files, with their cards, in a folder you choose. Browse them by day, sort them into folders, tag them, and ask a past session a question.

Features

  • Answers, context and fact-checks

    Cards appear by themselves at the point in the conversation they relate to. Choose how often, and which kinds.

  • Ask anything

    Type a question at any time, or right-click a line of the transcript to answer, explain or fact-check it.

  • Any call, video or room

    It hears your microphone and your Mac's audio, so it works with any meeting app, a video, or people in the same room.

  • Your own sources

    Web search is built in. Advisor also searches the connectors your Claude or Codex setup already has, such as Jira, Slack or your notes.

  • Beside your call

    Keep the window on top, hide the transcript to see only the cards, and pause and resume without losing the session.

  • Summaries and notes

    Summarise a conversation in one click, and add your own notes to the transcript for Advisor to take into account.

Privacy

There are no Advisor accounts and no Advisor server. Research uses your own Claude or Codex subscription.

  • Transcription runs on your Mac. Audio never leaves it and is never saved.
  • Only transcript text is sent, and only to the AI provider you sign in with.
  • Advisor only listens after you press Listen.
  • Research agents can search and read, but can't write files or read credential folders such as ~/.ssh (details for each provider).
